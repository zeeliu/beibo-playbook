# Buyers · 客户类型

Read the card before the call. One card per buyer type. **Is the lead worth pursuing?** See the 5 checks at the bottom.
沟通前看对应卡片。**这个线索值不值得跟？** 见底部五项检查。

---

### 🏛️ National / state reserve agency · 国家/国有储备机构
*Examples: Kenya NCPB, Tanzania NFRA, Algeria OAIC, Nigeria strategic silos*
- **They care about:** food security, losses, staff safety, audit trail, political visibility. 粮食安全、损耗、人员安全、可追溯、政治关注
- **Lead with:** our Sinograin and national-reserve track record, monitoring, access control. 中储粮背景、监测、门禁
- **Ask:** Which sites are being built or upgraded? Is there a tender? Who is the EPC? 哪些库点在建或改造？有招标吗？EPC 是谁？
- **Watch out:** slow, tender-driven, intermediaries wanting a cut. Route via a local partner or the EPC. Never promise anything about a tender. 周期长、靠招标、中间人多；走当地伙伴或 EPC，不对招标做任何承诺

### 🌾 Co-op / commercial grain handler · 合作社/商业粮储企业
*Examples: Brazilian co-ops, US elevators, private storage operators*
- **They care about:** grain quality = price, loss, labor, ROI. 品质即价格、损耗、人工、回报
- **Lead with:** monitoring + aeration control and the loss-prevention math. 监测 + 智能通风，算损耗账
- **Ask:** How many sites and how big? New build or modernization? Who's your silo or automation supplier? 几个库点多大？新建还是改造？仓体或自动化供应商是谁？
- **Watch out:** they compare with local suppliers on service. Ask about local support early. 会拿本地供应商比服务，早问本地支持

### 🏭 Flour miller / grain importer · 面粉厂/粮食进口商
*Examples: large millers with port silos*
- **They care about:** incoming grain quality, fast turnover, fumigation compliance. 进粮品质、周转、熏蒸合规
- **Lead with:** monitoring, gas detection, recirculation fumigation, inbound/outbound. 监测、气体检测、环流熏蒸、出入库
- **Ask:** How much raw grain is held, and for how long, before milling? 磨粉前存多少原粮、存多久？
- **Watch out:** if they store only flour or processed product, it's not a fit. 只存面粉或成品的不适配

### 🏗️ EPC / silo builder / integrator · 总包/建仓商/集成商
*Examples: Chinese EPCs building reserves in Africa, local automation firms*
- **They care about:** a reliable subsystem that's easy to integrate, margin, on-time delivery. 可靠易集成的子系统、利润、按时交付
- **Lead with:** *"You build the silo, we supply the brain inside it."* 他们建仓体，我们做仓里的脑子
- **Ask:** Which projects are coming up? Who specifies monitoring today? 有哪些项目？现在监测由谁指定？
- **Watch out:** technical integration questions come fast, so most of them are 🔴. Bring Zee in early. 集成技术问题多，大多是 🔴，尽早拉 Zee 进来

### 🤝 Agent / distributor · 代理/经销商
- **They care about:** margin, exclusivity, product they can actually sell and support. 利润、独家、能卖能服务的产品
- **Lead with:** our product range and 7,000+ installations; ask about *their* customers and technical team. 产品线和 7,000+ 安装；问他们的客户和技术团队
- **Ask:** Which grain-storage customers do you already serve? Do you have installers? 已服务哪些粮储客户？有安装人员吗？
- **Watch out:** "exclusive government access" with no named site or budget. Never promise exclusivity or commission. 声称有政府关系但说不出具体项目；绝不承诺独家或佣金

## Is it worth pursuing? 5 checks · 值不值得跟：五项检查

Mark each ✅ / ❌ / ❓ in your [call notes](templates/call-notes.md).

| Check · 检查 | ✅ Yes | ❌ No |
|---|---|---|
| **Raw grain** 原粮 | Wheat, maize, paddy, barley, sorghum | Only flour, milled rice, feed. Soy/oilseeds → ask Zee |
| **≥5,000 t fixed storage at one site** 单库点 ≥5,000 吨固定仓 | Fixed silo or flat warehouse | Silo bags, small bins, only a national total |
| **Something happening now** 当前有进展 | New build, expansion, upgrade, tender | "Maybe someday" |
| **Real buyer route** 决策路径清楚 | Named owner, co-op, operator, EPC | Only a ministry name |
| **Payment looks possible** 付款可行 | Clear payer and currency | Unclear FX or payer, integrity red flags |
