# Follow-up messages · 跟进消息

Short and personal. Replace the [brackets]. Send in the client's language where possible.
简短、个性化，替换 [方括号] 内容，尽量用客户的语言。

### After first contact (WhatsApp) · 首次接触后
> Hi [Name], this is David from Beibo. Thanks for your time today. As promised, here's our 2026 catalog. To suggest the right setup for your [silos/warehouses], could you share the grain type, the number and size of your stores, and whether this is a new build or an upgrade?

### After an expo · 展会后
> Hi [Name], great meeting you at [expo] in [city]. You mentioned [their pain, e.g. "checking grain temperature by hand"]. That's exactly what our monitoring systems handle at 7,000+ installations in China. Attached is our catalog. Would a short call next week work to look at your site?

### When you owe an answer · 欠客户答案时
> Hi [Name], thanks for your question about [topic]. I'm confirming the details with our engineering team and will come back to you by [day].

### No reply after 7 days · 7 天未回复
> Hi [Name], just following up on our conversation about [site/project]. Is [project] still moving forward this year? Happy to help whenever the timing is right.

### Handing over to Zee · 转交 Zee
> Hi [Name], I'd like to introduce Zee Liu, who leads our overseas business. Zee will prepare the proposal for your [project] and can answer the technical details.
