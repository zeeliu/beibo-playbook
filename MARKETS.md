# Markets · 市场优先级

From the 2026 market-entry memo and the Nairobi trip. **Zee sets priorities; this page reflects them.**
依据 2026 出海决策备忘录和内罗毕考察。**优先级由 Zee 定，本页同步。**

| Priority | Market · 市场 | Who to talk to · 找谁 | Note · 备注 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Brazil** 巴西 | Co-ops, commercial grain handlers, local automation/silo partners | Storage finance is active. Strong local suppliers (e.g. Kepler Weber), so position us as the monitoring/control layer. Portuguese. 有仓储融资；本地供应商强，主打监测控制层；葡语 |
| 1 | **Algeria** 阿尔及利亚 | OAIC programme via local EPC / legal partner | 350 × 5,000 t local storage centres being built. Tender-driven and slow. French/Arabic. 在建 350 个 5000 吨库点；招标、周期长；法语/阿语 |
| 2 | **Nigeria** 尼日利亚 | Silo concessionaires, private millers and importers | Policy names CO₂ sensors in silo bins. High payment and FX risk, so every deal goes through Zee. 政策点名 CO₂ 传感器；付款外汇风险高，一律经 Zee |
| 2 | **Kenya** 肯尼亚 | NCPB (met at Nairobi 2026), farmer-community leaders, private storage owners | NCPB relies on manual monitoring today. Much private storage isn't findable online, so relationships matter. NCPB 目前靠人工；很多私人仓网上查不到，靠人脉 |
| 3 | **India** 印度 | PPP operators and integrators (not FCI directly) | Huge but locally controlled. 体量大但本地把控 |
| 3 | **Tanzania** 坦桑尼亚 | NFRA via local partner | Smaller, public, existing Chinese EPC presence. 规模较小，公共项目，已有中国 EPC |
| Later | **USA, Europe, Türkiye** 美、欧、土 | Distributors / technology partners only | Mature markets with strong incumbents. 成熟市场，竞争强 |
| Avoid | **Argentina** 阿根廷 | — | Mostly silo bags. Not a fit. 主要用筒仓袋，不适配 |

## Channels that work · 有效渠道
- **Chinese EPCs and silo builders already working abroad:** "they build the silo, we supply the brain." 已在海外建仓的中国 EPC
- **Henan Aohua (河南奥华)**, grain-processing lines: bundle opportunity in Africa and SE Asia. Someone else at Beibo owns this relationship. Check with Zee before contacting. 加工线可打包；有专人负责，联系前先问 Zee
- **Provincial trade delegations / chambers of commerce:** Henan Dept. of Commerce subsidizes overseas expos. 省商务厅/商会组团，有展会补贴
- **Pre-booked meetings at expos** beat walk-ins. 展会提前约见比现场等客有效
