## 5 rules · 五条铁律

1. **No prices, discounts, delivery dates or payment terms from you.** Those come from Zee. 价格、折扣、交期、付款条件一律由 Zee 出
2. **Every system is configured per site.** Always ask the storage type, size and grain first. 每套系统按现场配置，先问仓型、规模和粮种
3. **Move the conversation to WhatsApp and send the 2026 catalog.** 把对话转到 WhatsApp，发 2026 产品目录
4. **Log every conversation within 24 h** ([template](templates/call-notes.md)). 每次沟通 24 小时内记录
5. **Anyone asking for commissions, gifts or side payments: stop and tell Zee.** 有人要回扣、礼品或私下付款，立即停止并告诉 Zee
