## Five systems + one platform · 五大系统 + 一个平台

| System · 系统 | Includes · 包含 | Problem it solves · 解决的问题 | Lead line |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Grain Condition Monitoring** 粮情监测 | Temperature/humidity cables, controllers, collectors, wireless monitors; pest traps and sampling; gas detection; inspection robot | Problems found too late 发现太晚 | "Know storage conditions at every point." |
| **Temperature Control** 控温 | Aeration control (fans, motorized windows, dampers, rain caps); internal-loop air circulation; grain-cooling units | Warm grain, manual or badly timed aeration 粮温高、通风靠人工 | "Automate the air path. Cool grain when the weather can't." |
| **Grain Protection** 粮食保护 | Recirculation fumigation; nitrogen controlled atmosphere | Uneven fumigation, chemical exposure 熏蒸不均、接触药剂 | "Protect grain evenly, with or without chemicals." |
| **Inbound & Outbound** 出入库 | Vehicle entry, weighing, sampling, records | No reliable load records 进出无可靠记录 | "Make every load visible." |
| **Storage Platform** 智能仓储管控平台 | Depot overview, per-silo view, equipment status, records, personnel | Data scattered or on paper 数据分散 | "One platform for the entire depot." |

**Safety & site equipment · 安全与辅助：** smart access control 智能查粮门禁 · integrated control cabinet 综合控制柜 · electric manhole & lifting platform 电动人孔及升降平台 · airtight doors 气密闭门

## What to lead with · 先讲什么
- **Default:** Monitoring. It's the easiest entry point and fits both new builds and retrofits. 默认先讲监测：最容易切入，新建改造都适用
- **Hot or humid climate:** Monitoring + aeration control + cooling. 热带/潮湿地区：监测 + 智能通风 + 冷却
- **Insect problems or fumigation complaints:** Pest & gas detection + recirculation fumigation or nitrogen CA. 虫害或熏蒸问题：虫气检测 + 环流熏蒸或充氮气调
- **Safety incidents or concerns:** Access control + inspection robot. 安全事故或顾虑：门禁 + 巡检机器人
- **Large multi-site operator:** Platform first, then modules per site. 多库点大型运营方：先讲平台，再按库点配模块

| grain cooling 谷物冷却 | air conditioning 空调 |
| aeration control 智能通风 | ventilation system (OK, but less precise) |
| controlled atmosphere 气调 | MA |
| installations 安装 | installation points 安装点位 |
| grain condition monitoring 粮情监测 | grain situation 粮情 (literal translation) |
